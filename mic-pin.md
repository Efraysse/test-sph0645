# Branchements du micro SPH0645
|Broche du sPH0645            | Broche correspondante sur l'ESP32 Wroom|
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------|
|3V                           | 3V ESP                                 |
|GND                          | GND                                    |
|BCLK (BIT Clock)             | GPIO 04                                |
|DOUT/DATA                    | GPIO 00                                |
|LRCL/ STB (Word Select)      | GPIO 02                                |
|SEL (Select)                 | GND                                    |
